How we coach

Four weeks, one thing at a time.

  1. 01

    Watch

    Your first session is filmed. You see your own footwork before anyone tells you about it.

  2. 02

    Focus

    We choose one thing to change for the month, usually the first step to the ball, and drill only that.

  3. 03

    Build

    Drills turn into games: the same shot under pressure, with a partner who is trying to win.

  4. 04

    Play

    You take it into Tuesday’s ladder league and we watch the match with you afterwards.
